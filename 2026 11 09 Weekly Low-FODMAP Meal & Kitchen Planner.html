HelloFresh Kit #23 • Monday Dinner & Tuesday Lunch

Prime Porterhouse Steak & Rosemary Butter with Sautéed Greens & Mash

Yield: 2 generous dinner serves + 2 reheat lunch portions
❌ Discard from Kit: Whole fresh garlic cloves.
✅ Retain from Kit: Thick-cut porterhouse steak, potatoes, baby broccoli, asparagus, lemon, rosemary, sea salt flakes.
🔄 Safe Swaps: Pure garlic-infused olive oil, plain butter, lactose-free milk, salt and black pepper.

Step 1: Potato Mash

Peel potatoes and cut into chunks. Boil in salted water for 12–15 minutes until fork-tender. Drain and mash with 2 tbsp plain butter, 3–4 tbsp lactose-free milk, salt, and pepper until silky smooth.

Step 2: Safe Rosemary Butter

In a small bowl, combine 20g softened plain butter with finely chopped fresh rosemary leaves, lemon zest, a pinch of sea salt flakes, and cracked pepper. Mash with a fork and set aside.

Step 3: Sear the Porterhouse Steak

Pat steak dry with paper towels. Season both sides with sea salt flakes and cracked pepper. Heat 1 tbsp garlic-infused oil in a heavy frying pan over high heat. Sear steak 3–5 minutes per side (medium-rare) holding upright with tongs to render fat strip for 1 minute. Remove steak to a warm plate, top with half the rosemary butter, tent with foil, and rest 5 minutes.

Step 4: Sauté Veggies

In the same frying pan, add baby broccoli and trimmed asparagus with a splash of water and a drizzle of garlic oil. Toss over medium-high heat for 5–6 minutes until tender-crisp. Squeeze lemon juice over top.

🍽️ Split & Tuesday Lunch Pack

Thinly slice steak across the grain. Plate half the steak, greens, and mash with the melting rosemary butter for dinner. Pack remaining half into containers for Tuesday work lunch (microwaves tender and delicious!).

HelloFresh Kit #3 • Wednesday Dinner & Thursday Lunch

Southeast Asian-Spiced Beef & Garlic Rice with Honey-Soy Veg

Yield: 2 dinner serves + 2 reheat lunch portions
❌ Discard: Garlic cloves, spice blend sachet, commercial honey-soy sauce sachet (high in fructose & onion/garlic).
✅ Retain: Beef strips, jasmine rice, green beans, Asian greens (bok choy/choy sum).
🔄 Safe Swaps: Garlic-infused olive oil, 2 tbsp tamari/soy sauce, 1 tsp pure maple syrup, 1/2 tsp rice vinegar, black pepper.

Step 1: Fragrant Rice

Melt 1 tsp plain butter with 1 tsp garlic-infused oil in a small saucepan. Add rinsed jasmine rice and a pinch of salt. Add water as instructed, bring to boil, cover with lid, and reduce to low for 10 minutes. Remove from heat and rest covered 10 minutes (fluffy, aromatic, zero high-FODMAP garlic!).

Step 2: Sauté Greens

Heat a splash of garlic oil in a large frying pan. Add trimmed green beans and roughly chopped Asian greens. Cook 3–4 minutes until tender-crisp. Transfer to a bowl.

Step 3: Beef Stir-Fry & Safe Sauce

Whisk together 2 tbsp soy sauce/tamari, 1 tsp maple syrup, 1/2 tsp rice vinegar, and 1 tbsp water. Coat beef strips with cornflour/plain flour, salt, and black pepper. Flash fry beef strips in screaming-hot garlic oil in batches (1–2 mins). Return all beef and greens to the pan, pour over the safe sauce, toss for 30 seconds until glossy.

🍽️ Split & Thursday Lunch Pack

Serve half over rice for Wednesday dinner. Pack second half for Thursday lunch (reheats beautifully in microwave). Reminder: Whisk Yogo Batch 2 tonight!

HelloFresh Kit #10 • Thursday Dinner & Friday Cold Lunch

Crumbed Chicken & Bacon Potato Salad with Green Beans & Mustard Mayo

✅ 100% Mushroom-Free! Tomatoes fully allowed.
❌ Discard: Garlic cloves & mustard mayo packet (often contains onion/garlic powder). Panko breadcrumbs if not GF (swap for plain rice crumbs or pan-sear plain).
✅ Retain: Chicken breast, bacon, potatoes, green beans, baby spinach, lemon.
🔄 Safe Dressing: 2 tbsp Kewpie mayonnaise + 1 tsp wholegrain/Dijon mustard + squeeze of lemon juice + salt and pepper.

Step 1: Boil Potatoes & Green Beans

Cut potatoes into bite-sized chunks and boil in salted water for 10–12 minutes. In the final 4 minutes, add trimmed green beans. Drain and allow to cool.

Step 2: Crisp Bacon & Cook Chicken

Slice bacon and cook in a large pan until crispy (5–6 mins). Remove bacon. Season chicken steaks with salt, pepper, and dried oregano. Pan-fry in garlic-infused oil (or air-fry at 200°C for 12–15 mins) until golden and cooked through. Slice into strips.

Step 3: Toss Potato Salad

In a large bowl, whisk 2 tbsp Kewpie mayo, 1 tsp wholegrain mustard, and lemon juice. Gently fold through the potatoes, green beans, baby spinach leaves, and crispy bacon.

❄️ Friday Lunch Pack Protocol (NO MICROWAVE)

Serve half warm or room temp for Thursday dinner. Pack the second half into an airtight container and chill in the fridge overnight. On Friday, eat straight cold—crisp schnitzel/tenders, salty bacon, and chilled tangy potato salad need no microwave whatsoever!

Comfort Batch Cook • Sunday Dinner & Monday Lunch

Sausage & Carrot Casserole with Fluffy Mash (Bean-Free)

Step 1: Creamy Mashed Potatoes

Peel 4–5 medium potatoes and cut into chunks. Boil in salted water 12–15 mins until fork-tender. Drain well, return to the pot, and mash with 2 tbsp plain butter, 3–4 tbsp lactose-free milk, salt, and black pepper until creamy.

Step 2: Brown Sausages & Carrots

Heat 1 tsp garlic-infused oil in a deep frying pan over medium-high heat. Brown 6 sliced Woolworths beef sausages for 4–5 mins until golden. Add 2–3 sliced carrots into the pan.

Step 3: Simmer Savory Gravy

Whisk together 2 cups boiling water, 2 tsp Massel 7 Beef stock powder, 1 tbsp soy sauce/tamari, 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1/2 tsp dried thyme, and 1.5 tbsp cornflour (dissolved in 2 tbsp cold water). Pour over the sausages and carrots. Cover and simmer gently for 15–20 mins until carrots are tender and the gravy is thick and glossy.

🍽️ Serve & Monday Lunch Pack

Ladle hot sausage casserole over fluffy mash for Sunday dinner. Pack remaining 2 servings into lunch containers for Monday 16 Nov work lunches!

Low-FODMAP High-Protein Formula

DIY YoPRO Chocolate Yogo Routine (6 Snack Pots @ ~130g each)

Only 2 tubs needed all week! Monash safety: 2.7g cocoa per pot (well under 8g safe threshold).

Ingredients per Batch (6 Pots)

  • • 1 × 700g Danone YoPRO Vanilla tub
  • • 5 level tsp pure cocoa powder (~16g total; Dutch-process or natural)
  • • 6–8 tbsp lactose-free milk (creates signature smooth pudding wobble)
  • • 1 pinch fine sea salt (vital: neutralizes lactic sourness)
  • • Optional topper: Fresh sliced strawberries just before packing

60-Second Whisk Method

  1. Add YoPRO vanilla yogurt, cocoa powder, lactose-free milk, and the pinch of salt into a mixing bowl.
  2. Whisk vigorously for 60 seconds until completely glossy, smooth, and lump-free.
  3. Divide evenly into 6 snack tubs (~130g each).
  4. Refrigerate. Keeps fresh and set for up to 4 days!
